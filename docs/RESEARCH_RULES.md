# Research and evidence rules

1. Use company-owned pages as the primary source for services, addresses, portfolio size and business contacts.
2. Search snippets are discovery hints, not definitive proof. Store them as secondary source records.
3. Never scrape LinkedIn pages or guess email patterns.
4. A generic inbox (for example, info@) is not a named contact.
5. Never infer managed units, metering equipment, incumbent supplier, contract dates or buying intent.
6. If a company website does not confirm a service, store `Unknown`; silence is not evidence of `No`.
7. Every material extracted claim should carry a source URL and a short source excerpt where possible.
8. Model output is untrusted until it passes deterministic checks against the fetched pages.
9. Scores rank public evidence and service fit. They do not certify a prospect as qualified or ready to buy.
10. Human review is required before using contact details for outreach.
